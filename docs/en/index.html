Free version

Want to try it on your computer first?

The free version is a Windows program. It already covers the basics of a repair shop, but it only works on the computer where you installed it.

  • Runs on your computer, no monthly fee
  • Up to 3 users
  • No access from phones or other devices
  • No WhatsApp, no tracking link and no N8N
⬇ Free download for Windows (85 MB)

Windows 10 or 11 · installs without an administrator password · takes a few minutes

How to install

  1. Download and save BancadaSync-Setup.exe.
  2. Double-click and follow the wizard. If Windows shows a warning, click More info and then Run anyway.
  3. Open BancadaSync and create your shop and your user.
Frequently asked questions

Still have questions?

Do I need to install anything for the online system?

No. Just log in from the browser on your computer, tablet or phone, with your e-mail and password.

When is the system activated after I pay?

As soon as the payment is confirmed. You create the account, confirm your e-mail and pay; activation is automatic.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter suits those just beginning, with up to 400 orders per month and you plus 2 employees. Professional is for shops with a team and WhatsApp, with up to 600 orders and 8 users. Business has no limit on orders or users.

What kind of repair shop is it for?

For shops that repair phones, notebooks, video game consoles, TVs and other electronics. You register the types of device your shop handles.

Is there a phone app?

It works in the browser and can be installed on the phone's home screen like an app, without going through an app store.

Is there a lock-in or contract?

No. Payment is month to month: you renew whenever you want, with no lock-in contract.

Can I try it before subscribing?

You can download the free version for Windows and try it on your computer. It works only on the computer where it was installed, for up to 3 users, and has no WhatsApp, tracking link or N8N.

What is the limit of the free version?

It runs only on the computer where it was installed, for up to 3 users, and has no WhatsApp, tracking link or N8N.

Why does Windows show a warning when installing the free version?

The installer does not have a paid digital signature yet (the “verified publisher” certificate), so SmartScreen shows “unknown publisher”. The file is the same one published here; check its integrity with the hash below.

Where is the free version's data stored?

On your computer, in %APPDATA%\CRM Assistencia. When uninstalling, the program asks whether you want to delete the data; the default is to keep it.

How do I check that the free version's file is the original? (SHA-256 of version 1.0.0)

1d10463c9d5ac24ce16623b04e5573c4cbf718de99053cb7d86f108681689fa7
In PowerShell: Get-FileHash .\BancadaSync-Setup.exe -Algorithm SHA256
